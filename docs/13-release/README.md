# 13-release: pilot, then launch

Phase S6 and S7.

## The pilot

A limited release with the design partner. Three things agreed in writing before it starts: how long
it runs, what success looks like, and what would make us stop. Kill criteria written afterwards are
worthless, because by then everyone is invested and the criteria move to fit the result.

Instrumented from day one. A pilot you cannot measure is a demo with a longer runtime.

## Launch

A support runbook, meaning written instructions for what to do when specific things break. An
escalation path with names on it. Sales enablement, pricing, documentation, and legal sign-off.

## The seasonality trap, which is specific to US retail

Retailers freeze system changes during their peak season, roughly October to January, because nobody
risks breaking anything while the stores are full. That means the window for buying and deploying is
narrow and comes once a year. Miss it and you wait twelve months. Plan the calendar backwards from it.

## The gates

For the pilot: did we hit the criteria we set beforehand, or did we move them?

For launch: if this breaks at 2am during peak season, who does what?

# NuAnchor: what it is, what it isn't, what we can reuse

## 1. What it does

Suniras, 9 Aug 2026, written without opening any deck or screenshot:

> "retail stores have to buy stock for seasonal sales months in advance, and this is a very tough job,
> which runs on old systems. NuAnchor helps you choose your products and volumes, and decide what they
> best places to buy from are."

**Claude's reaction: passes.** It gets the timing, the difficulty, and both outputs. Two things are
missing.

"Old systems" understates it and loses the detail that makes it vivid. The pack's own version is
spreadsheets and a price a factory emailed you. That is more specific and more damning.

The larger omission is the wedge. The pack argues that what makes NuAnchor different is pricing import
duty exposure at the moment the order is placed, because duty stopped being a number you look up and
started being a number that moves. That is the part the decks say no competitor at this customer size
does.

Neither omission matters now, because of section 3. But the pattern is worth noticing: the answer
describes what the product does and not what makes anyone choose it. Those are different questions, and
the second one is the one a positioning statement has to answer. It will come back at Phase 5.

---

## 2. What it does not do

Established across D-001 and the capability inventory, so recorded rather than re-argued.

No integrations, everything moves by spreadsheet. No security, tenancy or data residency detail in any
source. No stated setup time or price. No customer, only three named targets. No voice, despite NuPlay
being a voice product. And the biggest claim in the pack, that it improves each season, cannot be proven
inside a season by the pack's own admission.

---

## 3. What transfers to hiring: nothing

Suniras, 9 Aug 2026, on this section:

> "don't take any inspiration from nuanchor"

Recorded as D-011, which works through why that holds up rather than just accepting it. The short version:

- Domain logic transfers at zero and always did.
- Patterns are copyable by any competitor, so reusing our own puts us ahead of nobody.
- Craft is about how this project runs, not what it builds.
- Platform is the only tier that could be a genuine head start, and that is a Phase S1 engineering
  question decided against NuStack and NuPlay, not by looking at NuAnchor.

**One narrow thing kept, and it is not inspiration.** Nurix has faced the question of where AI belongs in
a decision and answered it explicitly twice: in NuAnchor's interface, per step, and in Unifi's screening
design. That is useful as precedent when we draw our own boundary at Phase 5 and defend it at Phase 7. It
is not a reason to build anything.

---

## 4. The Walmart question

Answered 9 Aug 2026. NuAisle does not sell to big-box or mass retail. The decks are right and the demo
narration was wrong.

Consequence, now a constraint rather than a question: high-volume frontline hiring concentrates in the
segment NuAisle skips, so either this product sits outside NuAisle or its target is mid-market retail.
Phase 4 decides. See Q-002.

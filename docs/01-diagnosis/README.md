# 01-diagnosis: what is broken, for whom, and why nobody has fixed it

Phase 1. This is where the project either gets real or stays a wish.

## What this phase is actually for

"Hiring is a bottleneck" is a symptom, not a diagnosis. A diagnosis names a specific step, for a
specific role, and says what it costs and who pays. Break the hiring funnel into stages and find where
the money and time actually leak:

sourcing, application completion, screening, scheduling, interview show-rate, offer, background check,
onboarding paperwork, day-one show-rate, then whether they are still there after 90 days.

Different roles behave differently. An hourly associate, a floor manager, and a store manager have
different requirements, different people doing the hiring, and different timelines. Say which one you
mean.

## The question this phase owes an answer to

Why hasn't this been fixed already? Fountain, Paradox and others have had ten years and real funding.
If both the problem and the solution were obvious, something structural is stopping it. Find out what.
That answer usually contains the opportunity, and skipping it is how you end up rebuilding something
that already exists.

## What goes in here

Funnel breakdowns with real numbers attached. Notes on where the cost sits. Written attempts at the
"why not fixed" question. Label every claim as verified, assumption, or unknown.

## The gate

Would a US retail recruiting leader read this and recognise their own problem, in their own words?

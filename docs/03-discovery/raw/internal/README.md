# internal: colleague conversations, not discovery

Raw notes from conversations with people inside NuPlay and Nurix. Same no-editing rule as the parent
folder. Kept separate for one reason only.

## These do not count toward the discovery gate

The gate in ../../README.md is: the same specific pain heard from **five people who do not work at
Nurix**. Nothing in this folder counts toward it. As of 25 Aug 2026 that count is still zero.

Colleagues are useful in a different way. They have read the market, met clients, and used the software.
That makes them a good source of leads and a bad source of proof. Every claim in here is at least
one hop away from the person who lives the problem, and usually two.

## Chain of custody matters here

Write down who said it to whom. A claim that reached this repo as "Anuj told Nishant, who told
Suniras" is weaker than a claim Anuj wrote down himself, and both are weaker than something a store
manager said. D-003 governs which source wins when they disagree. D-005 governs what Phase 1 is
allowed to run on.

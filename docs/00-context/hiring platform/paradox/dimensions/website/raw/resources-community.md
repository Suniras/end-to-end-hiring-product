## `/the-conversation` — content hub

Branded interview series, "talks with HR leaders." Mixed formats: blog interviews (3-6 min reads),
webinars (8-31 min), video conversations. Guests are VP/Director-of-TA-level executives at named customers
(Marriott, Medtronic, Compass Group, Ace Hardware, 7-Eleven, etc.) plus industry analyst Josh Bersin.
Themes: implementing hiring tech at scale, internal buy-in, quality-of-hire, franchise/high-volume hiring,
"how AI in hiring preserves culture." Functions as a customer-marketing/thought-leadership engine layered
directly on top of the case-study roster — nearly every "Conversation" episode maps to an existing
case-study customer.

## Fountain Source — /source

**Headline:** "24/7 Agentic AI that actively manages your sourcing spend"

- "Eliminate wasted spend with always-on optimization"
- "See exactly which channels drive your best hires"
- "Fill roles faster by focusing on what works"
- "Share real results with talent, operations, and finance teams"
- Unified tracking across job boards + campaigns; real-time channel analytics; automatic budget adjustments based on performance
- Integrates with Hire, Pool, Onboard "in Frontline OS"
- Role-based views for recruiters/managers/finance

**Claimed results:** 20% reduction in sourcing spend · 15% faster time-to-hire (high-volume roles) · 3x improvement in hire quality when optimizing with Source data.

**Integrations named:** Accurate Background, ADP Workforce Vantage, Equifax, Indeed, SAP, Testlify, UKG, Workday.

## Fountain Hire — /hire, /enterprise-ats

**Headline:** "Fast, flexible, and built for frontline teams" / "10x your Hiring Speed with Agentic Hiring"

- "Cut time-to-hire so you can meet demand without burning out your team"
- "Tailor every hiring workflow to fit different roles, locations, and compliance needs"
- "Increase applicant volume with fewer drop-offs and faster follow-ups"
- "Keep candidates moving with AI that handles questions and tasks"

Capabilities: mobile-friendly application flows without sacrificing screening; auto-screening; self-service interview rescheduling; "agentic follow-up" (AI nudges candidates through the funnel); centralized dashboard; workflow customization per location; compliance/regulatory flexibility.

**Claimed scale:** 91M+ applicants processed, 14M+ hires, 75+ countries.

**Security/compliance claim on this page:** "SOC 2 certified, global compliance support, transparent AI reporting." (This is the only page in the crawl that explicitly states "SOC 2 certified" as a bare claim — the dedicated /security page does NOT name SOC 2 explicitly in the extracted text, only "third-party security audits" — flag as a claim needing cross-check, e.g. via a Trust Report / SOC 2 report link.)

## Fountain Onboard — /onboard

**Headline:** "Get new hires to day one 2x faster with fewer errors"

- "2x improvement in compliance task completion rates"
- "40% faster onboarding completion"
- "30% reduction in Day 1 no-shows"
- Customer quote: "It used to take us five days to get someone fully onboarded. Now it takes one."

Features: mobile-first for hourly workers; digital forms/document collection/e-signature; task tracking + automated reminders; rules-based workflows by location/role/worker type; audit logs + completion reports; multi-language task completion; live dashboards.

**Security claim:** "SOC 2-certified with full audit trails."

Use cases named: warehouses, restaurants, retail brands, QSR chains, logistics companies.

## Fountain Shift — /shift

**Headline:** "Smarter scheduling for the frontline workforce" / "10x Your Shift Coverage with Agentic Scheduling"

- "Match the right people to the right shifts, fast"
- "Eliminate manual scheduling headaches"
- "Ensure reliable coverage with location-verified clock-ins" (implies geofenced/location-verified time clock — a concrete technical capability, not just "AI")

Capabilities: custom rules for shift logic/breaks/overtime/location coverage; mobile self-service shift request/pickup/drop; "agentic AI that identifies coverage gaps and recommends fixes"; audit logs, shift history, enterprise-grade permissions.

**Claimed results:** 40% reduction in scheduling-related absenteeism · 80% faster shift coverage during peak week · 30% reduction in labor cost variance.

Target use cases: retail chains (peak/off-peak), logistics/delivery hubs (24/7 coverage), hospitality groups.

## Fountain Pool — /pool (the "CRM" module)

**Headline:** "Reconnect. Rehire. Repeat."

- Rehire past applicants "in a few clicks"; converts "silver medalists" or former workers into candidates
- "Always-on talent visibility" across everyone who has interacted with the brand
- Engagement triggers based on skills match, location need, role openings, seasonality
- "No bloat, just action" — positioned as simpler/less-configuration than a traditional CRM

**Claimed results:** 35% reduction in time-to-fill using existing candidates · 60% average savings on sourcing spend · 4x re-engagement rate vs. manual recruiter outreach.

## Fountain Assist — /assist (a MANAGED SERVICE, not self-serve software)

**Headline:** "The hiring service that does the hard work for you"

This is Fountain's outsourced/RPO-style offering — Fountain's own team (not the customer's recruiters) sources and screens candidates:
- "We do the sourcing and screening for you"
- "Pre-qualified, ready-to-interview workers within 48 hours"
- "Our team uses AI to source, screen, and schedule qualified workers fast" — explicitly **human team + AI augmentation**, not autonomous agents
- "Transparent per-hire pricing," "No upfront costs" (only qualitative pricing signal on the whole site)
- "95% interview attendance rate"; replacement guarantee if a hire doesn't show
- Fully integrated into the customer's Fountain dashboard

# 03-discovery: talking to actual people

Phase 3. Everything before this is desk research. This is where you find out whether the diagnosis
survives contact with people who live the problem.

## Who to talk to

US retail recruiting leaders, recruiting operations staff, district managers, store managers, and
candidates. Store managers matter more than they look. They are often the person who decides whether a
tool actually gets used, which decides whether the contract renews.

## The Mom Test

Ask about what people already did, never about what they would do. "Walk me through the last time you
had to fill a role in under a week" gets you facts. "Would you use a tool that screens candidates by
voice?" gets you politeness. People are consistently bad at predicting their own future behaviour and
consistently reliable at describing last Tuesday.

## Keep raw and synthesis apart

raw/ holds what people actually said, unedited, one file per conversation. synthesis/ holds what you
think it means. The reason for the split is that interpretation drifts over months while raw notes do
not, and when a finding gets challenged you need to go back to the words.

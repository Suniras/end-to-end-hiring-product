# Partners — the overlay-onto-incumbent-ATS GTM pattern

This is the load-bearing artifact for the "layers onto Workday/SAP/Indeed rather than replacing them" GTM
question. **Confirmed clearly across all three partner pages** — same pattern this project has seen before
(Quinyx's AI-optimization overlay onto UKG/Infor in the Store-Ops batch).

## Paradox for Workday (`/partners/workday`)

**Positioning (verbatim):**
- "Paradox uses conversational AI to provide simple, frictionless hiring experiences built to **improve
  Workday Recruiting**"
- "Simple, frictionless experiences — built to **make Workday better**"
- "Enhance your entire hiring lifecycle **without replacing your system of record**"

**Integration points:** two-way SMS texting through Workday · AI pre-screening · automated interview
scheduling (calendar integrations: O365, Exchange, Gmail, Teams) · automatic candidate-status sync back into
Workday Recruiting · a **browser extension** to text/schedule without leaving Workday · career-site
chat/text-to-apply · 24/7 candidate Q&A.

**Certification claim:** "Workday Certified" badge; product line explicitly named "**Paradox for
Workday**"; "Workday Certified for its reliability and accuracy." Security: SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001 (same
certs as the general security page).

**Stats:** GM saved ~$2M on admin tasks in <1 year · Johnson Controls answered 190,000+ questions,
scheduled 10,000+ interview requests, 89% application completion.

## Reconciliation / read

Paradox's entire partner GTM is explicit and repeated near-verbatim across 4 independent pages: it never
positions itself as an ATS/HCM replacement. It is a **conversational engagement + scheduling + screening
layer** that reads/writes status into the incumbent system of record (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, or a
long tail of ATS/HCM vendors), plus a channel-distribution layer onto Indeed. The two deepest, formally
certified partnerships are Workday ("Workday Certified") and SAP (a paid-tier "SAP Endorsed App" on SAP
Store) — both enterprise HCM incumbents Paradox explicitly says it will not replace. This is the same
"AI-optimization overlay onto the incumbent system of record" GTM shape as Quinyx-onto-UKG/Infor from the
Store-Ops batch, applied to talent acquisition instead of workforce scheduling.

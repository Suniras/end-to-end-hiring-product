## Detailed case studies (fetched in full)

### Stitch Fix
- **Headline:** "Increased applicants who pass background checks and show up on day one by 40%"
- 40% increase in applicants passing background checks + showing up day one
- Median time-to-hire reduced from ~3 weeks to 9.16 days
- Applicant conversion rate: 68% → 95%
- Quotes: "Customization is one of the main reasons we decided to stick with Fountain." — Haley Pike, Director of Recruiting. "Automation helps us reduce time-to-fill and drop-off rates, so we have less pressure from competitors." — Rachel Carey, Recruiting Coordinator Lead.
- Features used: drag-and-drop workflows for A/B testing hiring processes, automated applicant screening/routing rules, automated interview scheduling, SMS communication, automated email campaigns, per-location role customization.

### Bojangles
- **Headline:** "Decreased time-to-hire for staff by 80%" (30 days → 5.8 days)
- 86% reduction in job board spending
- 230 recruiting hours saved in 2022 through automated messaging
- 50% increase in application flow rate
- ~30% hire conversion rate with Text-to-Apply
- ~500,000 automated messages sent to candidates in 2022
- Expanded to manage hiring across **750 Bojangles locations**
- Quote: "I want Fountain on my side" — Jacob Salvo, Senior Director of Talent Management (re: future AI capabilities)
- Features used: Text-to-Apply, automated messaging, Indeed job-board integration, knockout questions/automated screening, routing rules, background-check integration (Orange Tree), prerecorded GM video messages, ATS workflow automation.

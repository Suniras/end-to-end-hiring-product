### Recruiters (fountain.com/role/recruiters)
**Headline:** "Find, engage, and hire the right candidates, faster."
- Positions itself explicitly as **"An ATS for hiring hourly workers"** — the plainest one-line category
  self-description found anywhere on the site.
- Addresses three named recruiter pain points: overwhelming task management, high turnover requiring
  continuous re-hiring, limited talent pools in competitive markets.
- Features: on-demand customization, "easy assessments" (skills/aptitude/culture fit), live + recorded
  video interviews, "advanced automation and AI" for applicant collection/scheduling, analytics.

## The explicit ICP statement (verbatim, repeated across home / why-fountain / hire / enterprise-ats)

> "Fountain is the AI-powered Superintelligence that sources, hires, and runs your frontline operations across every location, 24/7."

Read literally: **multi-location, high-volume, hourly/frontline-workforce employers** — the company
self-identifies as built for hourly workers specifically ("most applicant tracking systems aren't built
for [hourly workers]" — from /about-us founding story), NOT a general-purpose ATS for salaried/
white-collar hiring. This is a consistent, disciplined positioning choice across every page crawled — no
page markets Fountain for corporate/professional/exempt hiring as a primary use case (though
"Professional Services" is a listed industry vertical and a "Corporate" angle exists in passing via the
enterprise-ats umbrella page).

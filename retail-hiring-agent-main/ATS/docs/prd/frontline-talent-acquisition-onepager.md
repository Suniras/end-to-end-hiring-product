# Frontline Talent Acquisition

## Overview

Retail frontline hiring is a conversion problem, not a supply problem. Applications-per-hire doubled to 95, yet time-to-hire remains 21-30 days. 94% abandon applications; 32% no-show interviews; top candidates leave the market in 48 hours. Existing HCM systems are desktop-first and recruiter-centric — structurally mismatched to a mobile-only, hourly workforce turning over at 60-76% annually.

## Core Use Cases

- SMS-first conversational application (sub-2-minute, no login or download)
- Instant eligibility screening against store-level shift demand, certifications, and compliance rules
- Candidate self-service interview scheduling with automated reminders
- Unified candidate identity across all stores — re-applicants and former employees resolved on contact
- Perpetual talent pool with auto-activation when positions open

## Agentic Use Cases

- **Screening agent** autonomously categorizes and routes candidates; store managers see only pre-qualified, pre-scheduled people
- **Communication agent** manages full lifecycle messaging — zero ghosting, definitive outcome within 48 hours
- **Pool activation agent** monitors openings against historical candidates and re-engages matches without human initiation

## AI-First Vision

Hiring shifts from episodic requisition fulfillment to a continuous, autonomous talent engine. Conversational AI structures candidate data from natural language. Scoring models calibrate against manager override patterns. Cross-store identity resolution turns reactive backfill into predictive workforce assembly — the store manager's role reduces to one 15-minute conversation with a candidate the system already qualified, scheduled, and briefed.

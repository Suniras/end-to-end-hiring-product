# Maki People — Product Research

**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Website:** https://www.makipeople.com

## Company Overview
- AI-powered conversational hiring platform that deploys autonomous agents to handle sourcing, screening, interviewing, and scheduling across the full recruitment funnel.
- Founded 2022 in Paris, France. Co-founders: Maxime Legardez, Paul-Louis Caylar, Benjamin Chino.
- Series A ($28.6M / ~€27.8M) closed Jan 2025, led by Blossom Capital with DST Global, Frst, GFC, and Picus Capital. Total raised: ~$35.3M.

## Product
- Maki positions itself as an "AI tier" that sits above your existing ATS. It deploys a squad of named AI agents—each handling a distinct hiring stage—to automate screening, assessment, and scheduling end-to-end.
- Key features:
  - **Riku** — Sourcing agent (candidate discovery)
  - **Shiro** — Skills screening agent: 300+ skill tests, custom questions, knock-out logic, top-of-funnel filtering
  - **Mochi** — Conversational screening agent: voice/video/text interviews, 45+ languages, 24/7 availability
  - **Ken** — Deep assessment agent: coding tests, aptitude tests, problem-solving simulations (add-on to Shiro)
  - **Kumi** — Scheduling agent: calendar integration (Google, Outlook), candidate self-booking, no-show reduction
  - Bias detection and reduction built into assessment design, scoring, and recommendations
  - Customizable questions, case studies, free-text, document upload, and video interactions
  - Psychometric science embedded in evaluations
  - Audited by New York state for bias (proven less biased than human reviewers on ethnicity, gender, age)
- Target customer: Enterprise and large mid-market. Notable clients include H&M, BNP Paribas, PwC, Deloitte, FIFA, Abercrombie, Capgemini (multiple Fortune 500).
- Deployment: Cloud SaaS, multi-tenant. Operates in 50+ markets globally.
- Key use cases: High-volume hiring, pre-screening at scale, skills-based assessment replacing CV screening, multilingual global hiring, interview scheduling automation.

## Tech & Integrations
- Known tech stack: Proprietary AI models for real-time candidate skill assessment. LLM-powered conversational agents (voice, video, text modalities). Psychometric science layer for evaluation calibration. Specific backend/infrastructure stack not publicly disclosed.
- Key integrations (30+ ATS integrations confirmed):
  - **ATS:** Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, Workday, iCIMS, Lever, Ashby, Avature, Teamtailor, Workable, Talos ATS, Welcome to the Jungle, Eightfold AI, Oracle, SAP SuccessFactors
  - **Calendars:** Google Calendar, Outlook
  - **Assessment:** 300+ built-in skill tests; 150+ expert-designed tests
  - **Communication:** Voice, video, text-based candidate interactions
- API availability: API-driven integration with ATS/HRIS systems. No public API documentation found.
- AI/ML approach: Conversational AI agents with adaptive interactions based on candidate profile. Proprietary models assessing 300+ skills in real-time. Bias auditing and calibration with increasing data. ISO certified, GDPR compliant, EU AI Act ready.

## Pricing
- Pricing model: Credit-based, pay per candidate interaction and per agent activated. Modular—activate only the agents you need.
- Tiers: Enterprise-focused; custom quotes required. Ken (deep assessment) is an add-on to Shiro (screening).
- Known price points: Not publicly listed. Contact sales for quotes.
- Free tier or trial: No free tier mentioned. Trial availability not confirmed.

## Competitive Positioning
- **Key differentiators:**
  - Multi-agent architecture (5 named agents) vs. single-tool approach of most competitors
  - Full-funnel coverage: sourcing through selection, not just assessment
  - Conversational AI with voice/video/text modalities in 45+ languages
  - Overlay model: works on top of existing ATS rather than replacing it
  - Strong enterprise traction with Fortune 500 logos
  - Bias auditability and regulatory readiness (GDPR, EU AI Act, NYC audit)

- **Strengths:**
  - Proven scale: 50+ markets, 80% automation of screening, 3x reduction in time-to-hire
  - 300% YoY growth in 2024 with major enterprise contracts
  - Multi-modal candidate experience (voice, video, text, document upload)
  - Strong compliance posture for regulated industries
  - Fast-growing team and well-capitalized ($35M+ raised)

- **Weaknesses / gaps:**
  - Platform stability issues reported (intermittent unavailability per G2 reviews)
  - Integration/email reliability problems noted by users
  - Limited assessment coverage for niche roles (no dedicated situational judgment tests, AI-specific tests, or typing tests)
  - Candidate experience can feel impersonal for some roles
  - Young product still under active development
  - No public API documentation or developer ecosystem
  - Pricing opacity—no self-serve tier, enterprise-only positioning

- **How they compare to an AI agent-first ATS:**
  - Maki is an assessment/screening overlay, not a system of record. An AI agent-first ATS would own the candidate pipeline end-to-end (source of truth for candidates, jobs, stages) while Maki depends on an underlying ATS.
  - Maki's multi-agent model is architecturally similar to an agent-first approach, but each agent is narrowly scoped to hiring stages rather than being a general-purpose orchestrator.
  - An agent-first ATS could go deeper on sourcing intelligence (proactive database scanning, passive candidate engagement) where Maki's Riku agent capabilities are less documented.
  - Maki's strength in structured assessment and bias auditing is a proven moat that an agent-first ATS would need to replicate or integrate.
  - The overlay model is both a strength (easy adoption, works with existing stack) and a weakness (no control over candidate data model, limited workflow customization).
